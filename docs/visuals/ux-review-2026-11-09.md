# ForceSignal UX review - 2026-11-09

Goal under review: *make it as intuitive as possible while giving valuable feedback to the player, so even new players can pick things up quickly.*

## How this was run

- API on `:8080` (Development), Vite dev server on `:6297`, branch `spike/pixi-map` working tree as found.
- Two isolated browser contexts driven by Playwright (Chromium):
  - **Blue Admiral**: owner, desktop 1366x900.
  - **Red**: joining player, phone 390x844 (touch, mobile).
- Rules profile: an invented one, `Review Table (invented)` (d6; beam faces 6->2, 5->1 unscreened and 6->1 at screen 1; band 10; torpedo reach 28, band 7, best 3+; 4 hull rows; fighters 20). It corresponds to no published game.
- Fleets: Blue had *Valiant* (beam cruiser, F/FS/FP), *Lance* (pulse torpedo destroyer), *Haven* (carrier, 1 bay) and *Wasp Flight* (fighter group). Red had *Crimson* (beam cruiser) and *Ember* (all-round light beam frigate).
- One whole turn was played: plot, lock, reveal, movement, alternating fire, cease fire, log, then advance to turn 2 and fighter launch.
- The driver is at `/tmp/claude-1000/-home-caleb-Projects-ForceSignal/85a74895-3b6b-4a7f-8266-450a800c8cc4/scratchpad/ux/drive.mjs` + `part2.mjs` (scratch, not committed).

Screenshots are in [`ux-2026-11-09/`](ux-2026-11-09/). Full-page captures taller than 5000px were scaled to 60% and colour-quantised to keep them small.

Severity key: **blocker**: a new player gets stuck or misreads the game state. **confusing**: they can recover, but only by guessing or reading the log. **polish**: friction or looks.

## Headline findings

1. **Feedback lands in the wrong place.** Every command's result, success or refusal, goes to one muted line at the top of the board (`main.tsx:1520`). The commands live at the *bottom* of the sidebar, so the player is usually scrolled away from that line. Pressing **Ready** with no rules profile looks like nothing happened ([09](ux-2026-11-09/09-refused-ready-before-profile.png)). Refusals are styled exactly like successes, and old messages ("Ship added to fleet.") stay on screen for six or more phases.
2. **Nothing tells a player what to do next or who the table is waiting on.** The phase is a small chip in the top bar. After Ready, Lock, or Reveal, neither device says "waiting for Red", "your turn to fire", or "the owner must press Advance Turn". The Movement phase is the worst case: after both reveals the turn just sits there until the owner happens to press **Advance Turn** ([49](ux-2026-11-09/49-red-revealed-phone.png)).
3. **A shot's result is not shown where the shot was taken.** After **Fire**, the console only says "FORE BEAM HAS ALREADY FIRED THIS TURN" ([58](ux-2026-11-09/58-fire-beam-result-inspector.png)). The dice, the hits and the damage only appear in the Log tab ("rolled 3,6,1,2,6 for 4 damage (2 armor, 2 hull)"). The map shows no damage on the target either ([84](ux-2026-11-09/84-turn-2-map.png)).
4. **All eight match commands are always visible, in every phase and for every role** (`main.tsx:1477-1504`). Lock, Reveal and Advance Turn show during fleet setup. **Ready** is still a primary button during Order Entry. The joiner sees **Advance Turn**, which the server refuses for non-owners (`InMemoryMatchService.cs:1589`).
5. **The owner can end the firing phase with ships still unfired, with no warning.** In an earlier run the log said "Blue Admiral fires next". The owner then pressed Advance Turn, and Valiant, Lance and Ember lost their fire without any prompt (`InMemoryMatchService.cs:1595-1605`).
6. **Bug: arc toggles are clipped on desktop.** In the weapon row, AS/AP/FP are hidden and cannot be clicked at 1366px, so you cannot build a port-firing mount ([zz-bug](ux-2026-11-09/zz-bug-arc-toggles-clipped.png)). Cause: `.weapon-row`'s last grid column is `minmax(92px, 0.7fr)` (`style.css:843-847`), five 34px toggles need about 190px, and `.arc-toggle-row` (`style.css:744`) does not wrap.
7. **The phone layout is a single 6,000-12,000px column.** The joiner's page is 6,067px tall right after joining ([11](ux-2026-11-09/11-joined-phone-full.png)) and 12,423px tall after fleet setup ([24](ux-2026-11-09/24-red-fleet-phone-full.png)). The room code and every match command sit at the very bottom. The legal banner and the "Profile" header fill the first screen.

## Step by step

### 1. Landing

| Shot | |
|---|---|
| [01 desktop](ux-2026-11-09/01-landing-desktop.png) · [03 phone](ux-2026-11-09/03-landing-phone.png) | Title, the "Unofficial companion" legal banner, then Display name / Create Match / Room code / Join Match, then two backup buttons. |

- **confusing**: Nothing says what the app does at the table or what the steps will be. "Synchronized orders and battle records" is the only hint. *Fix:* add a 3-4 step strip under the form ("Create → share code → each player builds a fleet → plot, reveal, fire"), plus a "How to play" link (see the help draft).
- **confusing**: Create and Join have equal weight and are interleaved. A joiner has to work out that they skip the first button. *Fix:* use two clear cards, "Start a new match" and "Join a friend's match", or tabs.
- **polish**: The legal banner takes about 25% of the desktop fold and about 40% of the phone fold before the form. On both widths the link chips (GAME-ICONS.NET, CC BY 3.0) overlap the line above, so the text looks struck through. *Fix:* collapse it to one line with a "Details" disclosure after the first visit. The text must stay reachable.
- **polish**: The landing status line reads "READY." This is meaningless to a new player.
- **polish**: Export Last Device Backup and Restore Match From Backup are full-width, outlined buttons that compete with the main path. *Fix:* move them under a "More options" disclosure.

### 4. Rules profile (owner)

| Shot | |
|---|---|
| [13 blank](ux-2026-11-09/13-rules-profile-blank.png) · [14 editor open](ux-2026-11-09/14-rules-profile-editor-open.png) · [15 imported](ux-2026-11-09/15-rules-profile-imported.png) · [16 applied](ux-2026-11-09/16-rules-profile-applied.png) · [17 joiner](ux-2026-11-09/17-phone-after-owner-profile.png) | The editor sits in the 280px sidebar (`RulesProfileEditor.tsx:81`). - **good**: The gap list ("Say how many faces the die has.") is exactly the right kind of feedback, and Import JSON works.
- **blocker (new players)**: This is the gate to the whole app, but it sits between "Table" and "Points per player" in the sidebar with no step number. A new owner has no reason to open it until Ready is refused off-screen. *Fix:* make it step 1 of a setup stepper (see Top 10 #6).
- **confusing**: Labels are terse and assume rulebook vocabulary: "Draws blood on", "Takes the system on", "Reach when the mount says none (mu)", "Chains on face". "mu" is never expanded. *Fix:* add a one-line plain explanation or tooltip per field, and spell out "measurement units (mu)" once.
- **confusing**: Optional groups (needles, salvo, point defence, fighters) are mixed in with the required ones. *Fix:* split them into "Required to play" (name, die, beam table, band, hull rows) and "Optional systems", collapsed.

- **polish (a11y)**: The torpedo field's accessible name "Band width (mu)" matches a search for the table's "Width". Automation tripped on this, and screen-reader users will too. Scope the table fields' labels ("Table width").
- **idea, policy-checked**: A new player with no rulebook at hand cannot try the app at all. Consider a clearly labelled **"Practice profile (made-up numbers, not from any game)"** button. It is consistent with the no-published-numbers rule because the numbers are invented, like `SMOKE_PROFILE`. Content owners should confirm it.
